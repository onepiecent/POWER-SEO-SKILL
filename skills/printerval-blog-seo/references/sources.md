# Sources and verification level

The research was carried out on **2026-10-06**. The working environment blocks direct access to `developers.google.com`, `ftc.gov`, `gov.uk` and similar sites (egress proxy), so the sources below were confirmed through **the search-result summaries of the official pages themselves**, not by reading the pages in full. Therefore:

- Level **S** = content confirmed through the search summary of the official page in this session. Before quoting it verbatim or using it for a legal decision, **open the original page and read it again**.
- Level **D** = checked on real export files published on GitHub, by running `kw_ingest.read_keywords` on them (the header, the value formats and what the ingest reads). It shows what a file looks like, not what the vendor means by a column: the meaning comes from the vendor's documentation (level S).
- Level **K** = from background knowledge, not re-verified in this session.
- Regulations and guidance change often; check the original page before relying on any provision.

## Pitfalls met during the research

- **Search results can be wrong about holiday dates.** Search summaries returned "Mothering Sunday 2026 is 19 March" and "Father's Day 2026 is Wednesday 21 June", both wrong (Mothering Sunday 2026 is Sunday 15 March; Father's Day 2026 is Sunday 21 June). That is why `occasion_calendar.py` **computes dates by rule** and has tests that check them against the real calendar.
- Printerval's self-reported figures and the Semrush data seen through snippets are inconsistent; do not use them as evidence.
- The readability threshold (Flesch-Kincaid ≤ 10), the link density (3-5 per 1,000 words) and the threshold of about 50 inbound links (Zyppy, correlational) are **[Convention]/[Research]** (correlational research).
