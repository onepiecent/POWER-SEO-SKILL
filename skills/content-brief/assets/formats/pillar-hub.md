# Format: pillar-hub

Use when: role = pillar (broad topic with several cluster posts: an occasion, an interest, a recipient, a know-how area).
Reader job: orient quickly, understand the landscape of the topic, and jump to the exact guide they need.

## Skeleton
- H1: the broad topic (primary keyword) + promise.
- Intro (BLUF): what the topic covers and how this page is organised.
- H2: Start here (decision help: "If you need X, read Y") - the first screen must help someone who reads nothing else
- H2 per cluster: 2-3 sentence summary with the key takeaway, then a contextual link to the cluster post (one link per cluster)
- H2: Timeline or checklist (for seasonal pillars: what to do when; no delivery promises)
- H2: FAQs
- "Last updated" with a real change log for seasonal refreshes.

## Rules
- The hub must add value beyond a link list: curated guidance, comparisons, decision rules.
- Do not duplicate cluster content; summarise and send readers on.
- Link to every cluster in the topic map exactly once; the cluster posts link back with descriptive anchors.
- Seasonal pillars keep the same URL and are refreshed every year; update the visible date only when the content truly changes (Google: do not artificially freshen dates).

## Experience prompts
- What are the 3 questions customers ask us most about this topic? (support)
- What would we tell a friend who only has five minutes?
- A pattern from our own data or work (aggregated, sourced).

## Product-slot guidance
- Up to one slot per cluster summary where natural; none in the intro.
- Slot syntax: [PRODUCT-SLOT: product idea | context: reader situation | why: why it helps this reader]
