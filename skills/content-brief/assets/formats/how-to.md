# Format: how-to

Use when: reader_need = how_to or solve ("how to wash a graphic tee", "how to size a t-shirt").
Reader job: complete a task correctly and avoid ruining something.

## Skeleton
- H1: "How to ..." with the result the reader wants.
- Quick answer (3-5 line summary of the steps) before any background.
- H2: What you need (tools, time, difficulty)
- H2: Step-by-step (numbered; one action per step; the reason in a short clause)
- H2: Common mistakes and how to fix them
- H2: Troubleshooting (symptom -> cause -> fix)
- H2: FAQs
- Byline, "Last updated", sources.

## Rules
- Steps must be accurate and safe; if material-specific, say which material.
- Care and sizing advice comes from care labels, manufacturer data or documented testing. Never promise outcomes ("will never fade").
- Printerval-specific statements (our fabrics, our print method) only from product-team documents: [DATA NEEDED: product team confirmation].
- Photos or diagrams where they reduce errors (alt text required).

## Experience prompts
- What goes wrong most often when customers do this? (support tickets, aggregated)
- What does the production or quality team recommend, and why? (named role, documented)
- A before/after or measurement we really have.

## Product-slot guidance
- At most two slots; only for items the reader needs to finish the task (e.g. a size reference for sizing guides).
- None in the quick answer.
- Slot syntax: [PRODUCT-SLOT: product idea | context: reader situation | why: why it helps this reader]
