# Format: ideas-list

Use when: reader_need = inspire without a gift angle ("family halloween costume ideas", "photo caption ideas").
Reader job: get a handful of good options fast, and know how to adapt one to their situation.

## Skeleton
- H1: primary keyword + angle.
- Intro (BLUF): what the list covers and who it suits.
- H2 groups by theme, difficulty, budget or group size. Under each, H3 or numbered items with one-line "why this works" and a practical tip.
- H2: How to make it your own (adaptation tips)
- H2: FAQs
- Byline, "Last updated", sources.

## Rules
- Quality over count: do not pad to a round number. Cut ideas that are interchangeable.
- Each item has a distinguishing detail (who it suits, what it needs, what can go wrong).
- Original wording; do not copy competitors' lists.

## Experience prompts
- Which ideas do customers actually ask for or choose most? (support or order data, aggregated)
- What practical problems come up (sizing across a group, matching colors, lead time)? Confirm timing facts with operations.
- A real example with permission (photo, quote from a customer message).

## Product-slot guidance
- Slots only where a product is the natural answer; at most one per theme group.
- Slot syntax: [PRODUCT-SLOT: product idea | context: reader situation | why: why it helps this reader]
