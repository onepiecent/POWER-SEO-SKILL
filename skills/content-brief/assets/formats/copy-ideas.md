# Format: copy-ideas

Use when: reader_need = copy_ideas (slogans, quotes, captions, card messages, name ideas).
Reader job: find words that fit a person, tone and place (shirt, mug, card, photo) and can be used right away.

## Skeleton
- H1: primary keyword + tone or occasion.
- Intro (BLUF): what is here and how to choose a line.
- H2 groups by tone or relationship (funny, sentimental, short and bold...). Under each, 8-15 lines, each short.
- H2: How to pick a line that fits (length, tone, audience, readability at print size)
- H2: How to personalize (names, dates, inside jokes)
- H2: FAQs
- Byline, "Last updated", sources.

## Rules
- Original lines only. Do not copy song lyrics, film lines, brand slogans or lines owned by others; attribute true quotations to the real source.
- Avoid phrases built on brand names or celebrity names (flag with claims-compliance-check).
- Print-size advice (character counts, line breaks) must come from the design team: [DATA NEEDED: design team limits].
- Tone must suit the occasion; keep grief and sensitive occasions respectful.

## Experience prompts
- Which kinds of phrases do customers choose most for this occasion? (aggregated, no personal data)
- What layout limits do designers apply for this product? (documented)

## Product-slot guidance
- Zero to two slots, e.g. near "how to personalize" where the reader needs a product that supports custom text.
- Slot syntax: [PRODUCT-SLOT: product idea | context: reader situation | why: why it helps this reader]
