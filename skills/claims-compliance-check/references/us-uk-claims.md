# Summary of claim rules (US/UK)

**Not legal advice.** Sources and verification levels (S = confirmed through the search summary of the official page on 2026-10-06, so open the original page before relying on it; K = background knowledge, not re-verified) are in `printerval-blog-seo/references/sources.md`.

## United States

| Topic | Key points | Level | Consequence for the blog |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reviews and testimonials** (FTC 16 CFR Part 465, in force 21 Oct 2024) | Bans fake reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones or ones from people who never used the product; bans paying for reviews on condition that they express a particular sentiment; insider reviews (managers, senior staff) must disclose the relationship; bans company-controlled "independent review" sites that pretend to be independent; bans some ways of suppressing or hiding reviews; bans buying or selling fake social-media indicators. Civil penalties are available | S | Do not write "customers love..." or quote reviews without real data; do not use AI to make up customer quotes; if sellers or staff write posts, disclose the relationship. |
| **Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule** | When advertising, there must be a reasonable basis for any stated or implied delivery time; if none is stated, the seller must believe it can ship within 30 days; if it cannot deliver on time it must seek consent to the delay (with the right to cancel and a prompt refund) | S | Every "arrives by Mother's Day" or "order by Dec 10" needs operational data that is confirmed, dated and conditional. |
| **Green Guides** (16 CFR 260; guidance, not a rule) | Do not use generic environmental-benefit claims ("eco-friendly") because they are very hard to substantiate; recycled/recyclable claims need specific conditions | S | State specific, measurable attributes; no bare "sustainable". |
| **Endorsement Guides** | A material connection between the endorser and the seller must be clearly disclosed | K | Affiliate/sponsored/gifted: disclose at the top of the post and near the relevant content. |
| **Made in USA** | A "Made in USA" claim requires that "all or virtually all" of the product is made in the US | K | Do not use it for POD goods made across several countries unless each product is confirmed. |
| **Trademarks** (USPTO) | A mark does not have to be identical to cause confusion if it is similar in sound, appearance, meaning or commercial impression and the goods are related | S | Franchise, band or brand names: ask legal before using them to sell or promote products. |

## United Kingdom

| Topic | Key points | Level | Consequence for the blog |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DMCC Act 2024: reviews** (CMA207/CMA208) | From April 2025: posting or commissioning fake reviews, incentivised reviews without disclosure, or presenting review information misleadingly are banned practices in themselves | S | As with the FTC: use only real reviews; if there is an incentive, say so; do not hide negative reviews to adjust the rating. |
| **Green Claims Code** (CMA) | Six principles: claims are truthful and accurate, clear, do not omit or hide important information, make fair and meaningful comparisons, consider the product's whole life cycle, and can be substantiated | S | Same as the Green Guides. |
| **CAP Code and advertisers' own websites** (ASA) | Since 2011 the CAP Code has applied to advertising on an advertiser's own website; the ASA asks whether the content is "directly connected with the supply of goods". Some blog posts on a company's site may be editorial content if they are not clearly aimed at selling. Ads must be recognisable as ads; objective claims must be substantiated | S | A UK post that mentions Printerval's own products should be treated as possibly within the CAP remit; claims need evidence. Confirm how to disclose with legal. |
| **Trade marks** (UK IPO) | A trade mark is refused if it is identical or similar to an existing one | S | As above. |

## Rule → action mapping

| Rule | What to do |
|---|---|
| delivery_promise | Remove it, or write `[DATA NEEDED: order deadline / delivery time confirmed by operations, confirmation date]`. Link to the delivery policy page instead of repeating the figures. |
| review_testimonial | Quote only review figures that have a source and a date; do not invent; do not use AI to make up customer quotes. |
| eco_general / eco_specific | State a specific attribute plus a certification or supplier document; drop the generic wording. |
| health_claim | Remove it; move to an emotional benefit or an experience. |
| made_in | Only when the supply chain confirms it for that exact product. |
| superlative_guarantee | "Our pick for X because Y" plus the criteria; avoid "guaranteed", "#1", "lowest price". |
| price_offer / policy_claim | Let the commercial or policy team supply it, with a date; do not put it in an evergreen post. |
| first_hand_claim | Add a real `[EXPERIENCE]`/`[DATA]` or rewrite. |
| disclosure | Disclose at the top of the post, clearly, near the relevant content. |
| ip_brand | Ask legal; remove it from titles, anchors and product descriptions until approved. |

## Operating notes

- The waiver marker `[CLAIM-OK: reason; approver; date]` is placed only by the **approver**.
- Re-check claims whenever a seasonal post is refreshed (policies, prices, delivery deadlines and reviews all change).
- Artist and band names also involve personality and publicity rights; that is outside the scope of the tool.
